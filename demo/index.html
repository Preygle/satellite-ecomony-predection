    3. Ghost-growth screen (live)

    New development whose activity (night-time light, points of interest, population) is low for how built-up it is becomes a candidate. The rule below decides which candidates are emerging (filling up) and which are ghost growth (not filling up).

    Review 3 rule: a cell is emerging only if its night-time light rose significantly faster than the established city (p ≤ 0.10). The whole city brightened after 2013, so the Review 2 rule called almost everything emerging.
