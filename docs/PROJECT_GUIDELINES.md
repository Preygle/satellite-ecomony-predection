**School of Computer Science and Engineering (SCOPE)**
**Fall Semester 2026–2027**

---

## 1. Course Context and Syllabus Basis

The course aims to provide hands-on learning in the design, development and
analysis of a suitable product or process, and to strengthen students'
technical skills in the chosen field.

### Course Outcomes

| CO | Course Outcome |
|---|---|
| **CO1** | Demonstrate professional and ethical responsibility. |
| **CO2** | Evaluate evidence to determine and implement best practice. |
| **CO3** | Mentor and support peers to achieve excellence in the practice of the discipline. |
| **CO4** | Work in multidisciplinary teams and provide solutions to problems arising in multidisciplinary work. |

---

## 2. General Guidelines

- Each project team shall consist of a **maximum of three members**. The team
  shall meet the guide regularly and maintain evidence of progress, feedback
  received and action taken.

- **All review comments and suggestions shall be recorded**, and the action
  taken shall be presented during the subsequent review.

- The implementation, results, presentation and report shall reflect the
  **actual work carried out**. Each member's contribution shall be clearly
  demonstrated during the reviews, and every student shall submit an
  individual final report highlighting their contribution.


- The project report shall be prepared **progressively, chapter by chapter**.
  The guide shall verify the near-final report during Review 4, and all
  corrections shall be incorporated before Review 5.

## 5. Project Report Structure

| Section | Minimum Content |
|---|---|
| **Preliminary Pages** | Title page; bonafide certificate; declaration; acknowledgement; abstract; table of contents; list of figures; list of tables; list of abbreviations, as applicable. |
| **Chapter 1 — Introduction** | Background and motivation; problem statement; objectives; scope; expected outcomes; organisation of the report. |
| **Chapter 2 — Literature / Patent Review** | Relevant literature and patents, wherever applicable; comparison of existing approaches; limitations; identified gap. |
| **Chapter 3 — Methodology / System Design** | Proposed methodology; architecture/system design; module description; algorithms/workflow/experimental design; tools, technologies and datasets. |
| **Chapter 4 — Implementation and Results** | Implementation details; testing and validation; results and observations; comparison and analysis; limitations. |
| **Chapter 5 — Conclusion and Future Work** | Summary of work completed; major findings; conclusion; limitations and future enhancements. |
| **References** | All cited sources in the prescribed referencing style. |
| **Appendices** | Additional results, screenshots, test cases, datasets/questionnaires, design/code evidence, publication/patent proof and individual contribution statement, as applicable. |

**Requirements easily overlooked**

- Review comments must be **recorded** and the action taken **presented** at
  the following review (§2). Review 3 allocates 1 mark and Review 4 allocates
  2 marks specifically to this.
- Review 4 item 9 awards 2 marks for a **research publication or patent
  submission**, and explicitly gives no marks without documentary proof.
  This needs lead time.
- Every student submits an **individual final report** stating their own
  contribution (§2).
- Significant **AI tool use must be acknowledged** (§2).
- **Similarity compliance** is assessed in the Draft/Report Review.
